# Take-home message

## Core message
GSI’s strongest potential contribution is a minimal, BOL-referenced, frozen geometric representation whose transferability and full computational cost are measured rather than assumed.

## Slide content

**Do not claim novelty from geometry or referencing alone.**

Ask instead:

> How much geometric information from a partial-discharge voltage–capacity trajectory is actually required for reliable cross-cell SOH estimation?

Position GSI around:

- minimal representation;
- BOL-referenced normalization;
- frozen Head/Tail geometry;
- low target adaptation burden;
- quantified end-to-end computational cost;
- physically motivated, mechanism-unproven interpretation.

$$
\text{contribution strength}
=
\text{evidence for the trade-off, not the feature name}
$$
