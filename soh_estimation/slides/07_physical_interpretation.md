# Physical interpretation has four evidence levels

## Core message
Trajectory-level consistency can motivate GSI physically, but it cannot by itself identify a degradation mechanism.

## Slide content

| Evidence level | What can be claimed |
|---|---|
| Empirical correlation | GSI covaries with SOH |
| Phenomenological / geometric | Aging accompanies local $V(Q)$ deformation |
| Electrochemical consistency | GSI-region evolution co-occurs with ICA/DVA evolution |
| Mechanistic proof | A specific mode such as LLI/LAM/SEI is directly identified |

**Reviewed literature mainly supports levels 1–3.**

$$
\text{aging}
\rightarrow
\text{electrochemical/trajectory evolution}
\rightarrow
\text{regional geometry change}
$$

**It does not establish:** $GSI\equiv LLI$, $LAM$, or $SEI$ growth.

## Evidence from literature

- Naha 2020 — capacity/resistance-related trajectory interpretation, but no direct degradation-mode measurement.
- Wen 2022 — IC peak evolution supports an electrochemical bridge, not mechanism identification.
- Jenu 2022 — ICA offers stronger diagnostic context but is noise- and rate-sensitive.
- Chen et al. 2024 — handcrafted features are phenomenological and the final deep model remains difficult to interpret.

## Gap / limitation

Without dedicated diagnostics, GSI can be physically motivated and electrochemically consistent but remains mechanism-unproven.
