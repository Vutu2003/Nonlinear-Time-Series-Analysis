# Supplementary Content Map

## 1. Scope and publication principles

This map freezes the writing architecture for **15 sections, 14 tables and 11 figure slots**. It is a writing plan, not Supplementary prose. The current manuscript is the publication reference; its numerical results, conclusions, window-length reporting and Simplex reporting remain fixed. The technical evidence source is `phase1/writting_manuscript/supplementary/supplementary_results_master_summary.md` (section references below use its numbering).

Evidence roles:

- **Primary:** dataset/QC provenance, nominal methods, PPS RQ1 evidence and complete seven-metric 60-s RQ2 statistics.
- **Robustness/sensitivity:** window length, labels, reconstruction, estimator settings and hypothetical repeated-session grouping. These reuse the dataset and are not independent replications.
- **Exploratory/supporting:** symbolic dynamics; optional adversarial-search detail and additional secondary-metric sensitivity summaries.

Primary inference remains at the **recording-session level**. Unknown participant-session linkage remains an explicit limitation. DET describes recurrence-line structure, LLE describes finite-window local divergence, and PPS rejection concerns only the tested noisy pseudoperiodic null. None establishes physical determinism or deterministic chaos.

Include manuscript-rounded values in publication tables; preserve underlying stored precision during table preparation. For a nominal reference repeated in a sensitivity table, use the fixed manuscript value and refer to the primary statistics table rather than duplicating statistics from a different analysis family. Label raw p and BH-adjusted q separately, distinguish effect-sign consistency from statistical support, and retain non-significant results in each selected table.

Internal audit findings remain working records. I02–I05 are not publication blockers; I07 is closed for this phase. I08 additions are optional, and I09 warrants at most a short useful implementation note. Document the duration convention associated with each displayed quantity without turning it into a warning or changing manuscript totals. Do not invent acquisition, KSS/video or participant-linkage metadata.

All paths are relative to the project root. Notebook cell/output indices are zero-based. Status describes **writing readiness**, not a demand for new analysis:

- **READY:** evidence and scope are settled.
- **READY WITH MINOR EDITING:** needs selection, formatting or caption preparation from existing outputs.
- **OPTIONAL DETAIL MISSING:** core content is ready; omitted optional detail is not required.
- **NEEDS HUMAN WORDING DECISION:** a narrow wording choice remains; no experiment is requested.

No new experiment or inferential recomputation is required by this core plan. New figure panels mean plotting existing summaries during the later production stage, not rerunning analyses.
